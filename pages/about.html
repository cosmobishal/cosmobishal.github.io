ABOUT

Astronomy is the thread. The questions are the fabric.

Bishal Neupane is an astronomy student, science educator and aspiring researcher interested in the places where astronomy meets physics, computation and the search for life.

Where this started

Interest in astronomy grew from looking upward and then trying to understand what the view actually meant. That curiosity gradually moved from sky watching to observational work, astronomy education and research projects built around public scientific data.

The strongest motivation is not the prestige attached to astronomy. It is the scale of the unanswered questions. A useful project can be small in size and still reveal something important about how evidence is built.

What matters in the work

Good science needs a connection between theory and practice. Equations should meet measurements. Models should meet uncertainty. A lesson should give a student something to test rather than only something to remember.

That perspective shapes both research interests and educational work, from observational activities and telescope construction to computational studies of exoplanets and cosmological models.
