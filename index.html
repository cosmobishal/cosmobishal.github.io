About Me

Passionate about understanding the cosmos and sharing that knowledge with the world

I'm an astronomy researcher working at the intersection of observational astronomy and computational science. My work focuses on finding and characterizing exoplanets – planets orbiting stars beyond our solar system – and understanding which of these distant worlds might be capable of supporting life.

Currently, I serve as Astronomy Coordinator at The Squad of Changemakers, where I develop educational programs that bring astronomy to students across Nepal. I also founded ASK - Astronomy Squad of Koshi, a student-led initiative advancing astronomical research and education in the Koshi region.

What drives me is the combination of cutting-edge research and community impact. Whether I'm analyzing data from NASA's space telescopes or teaching students to observe the night sky, I believe astronomy has the power to inspire wonder and scientific curiosity in people of all backgrounds.

Research Focus

My work spans multiple areas of modern astronomy and computational science

Exoplanet Science

I study planets beyond our solar system using data from missions like JWST, TESS, and Kepler. My research focuses on identifying potentially habitable worlds and understanding what makes a planet capable of supporting life.

Machine Learning in Astronomy

Using AI and machine learning to analyze massive astronomical datasets. I work with techniques like MCMC fitting and neural networks to find patterns in data that would be impossible to detect manually.

Atmospheric Studies

Analyzing the atmospheres of exoplanets to search for biosignatures – chemical signs that might indicate the presence of life. This involves spectroscopy and radiative transfer modeling.

Multi-messenger Astronomy

Participating in gravitational wave follow-up observations and coordinating multi-wavelength campaigns to understand cosmic events from different perspectives.

Quantum Computing Applications

Exploring how quantum computing could revolutionize astrophysical simulations and data processing, opening new possibilities for solving complex astronomical problems.

Science Education & Outreach

Developing programs that make astronomy accessible to students in Nepal and beyond. Building the next generation of scientists through hands-on learning and mentorship.

Research Publications

Contributing to our understanding of exoplanets and habitability

Analysis of Exoplanet Habitability – Investigating Key Parameters and Environmental Influences

Neupane, B.

Zenodo | Published April 30, 2025

This research brings together data from multiple space missions – including JWST, TESS, and Kepler – to understand what makes a planet habitable. Using statistical modeling and machine learning, I examined how factors like stellar radiation, planetary size, and atmospheric composition work together to create environments where life might exist.

Identification and Characterization of an Exoplanet Candidate Using Multi-Mission Photometry and Spectroscopy

Neupane, B.

Zenodo | Published April 21, 2025

In this study, I identified and characterized a mini-Neptune candidate using transit photometry. By combining data from multiple missions and using MCMC fitting techniques, I was able to determine the planet's fundamental properties. The simulated atmospheric analysis suggests the presence of water vapor and methane – chemicals that tell us about the planet's composition and history.
