01 · ABOUT

A portfolio built around curiosity with evidence.

Physics gives the language. Astronomy gives the scale. Research gives the discipline. Teaching keeps the questions connected to people.

A

Between telescope and notebook

Interests move across observational astronomy, astrophysics, cosmology, astrobiology and computational methods. Some projects begin with a telescope or a public data archive. Others begin with a physical question that needs mathematics before it needs hardware.

There is a parallel commitment to science education. Astronomy becomes more meaningful when students can build, observe, calculate and argue from evidence rather than only memorize the names of objects.
